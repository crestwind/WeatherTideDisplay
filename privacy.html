Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Tidal Weather is a free weather and tide display. It's built to know as little about you as possible. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps, what it sends, and to whom.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads.
  • No cookies, no tracking, no analytics companies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your settings, including your location, stay in your own browser.
  • To show the weather, your browser asks public weather services for the forecast at the place you chose.
  • Feedback you send is emailed to the site owner and not stored anywhere else.

What's kept on your device

Tidal Weather saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between visits. They never leave your device unless noted below:

  • Your settings: the place you picked (its name and map coordinates), units, clock, display style and other choices, and the sound volume.
  • Saved data to load faster: recent weather history, tide tables and the list of tide stations, so they don't have to be downloaded every time.
  • Alert history: the weather alerts shown in the last 30 days, for the "Alert history" list in settings.
  • Visit dates: the date of this device's first visit and its most recent one (see "Visitor counts" below).
  • Small reminders: for example, which banner message the little plane flew last.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings. Tidal Weather then starts fresh, as on a first visit.

Your location

Tidal Weather never asks for your device's GPS or exact location. You type a town, city or ZIP code, and it finds that place's map coordinates. Those coordinates are what's used to get your weather, and they're saved only in your browser.

Services your browser contacts

The weather, tides and alerts come straight from public services. Your browser contacts them directly, not through us. Like any website you visit, each of them can see your device's internet (IP) address and the request it was sent, which usually includes the coordinates of the place you picked. Tidal Weather does not send them your name, email or anything else about you, and it tells them not to report which page sent the request.

Moon phases are worked out on your device, so nothing is sent for those.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub may briefly log the internet addresses of visitors for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Visitor counts

To know roughly how many people use the site, once a day each device sends a single word to our counter on Cloudflare: "new" on its first visit, or "returning" after that. The counter adds one to that day's total and keeps nothing else: no internet address, no location, no device details. Robots and automated visits are left out of the totals. Nothing is counted if your browser blocks site storage.

Feedback

If you use "Send feedback" in settings, your message is passed through our small program on Cloudflare and emailed to the site owner. If you add your email address, it's included so the owner can reply. Nothing is stored on the way. The email stays in the owner's inbox and is used only to read and answer your message.

Children

Tidal Weather doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The feedback form is optional. If a child has sent us an email address, contact us and we'll delete the message.

Changes to this policy

If what the site keeps or sends ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Questions

Use Send feedback in Tidal Weather's settings (the button in the bottom right corner of the screen).
